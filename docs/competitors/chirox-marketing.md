## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: Premium search-only marketing focused purely on capturing high-intent traffic to generate actual booked appointments rather than vanity metrics.

**Target audience**: Established chiropractic businesses ($1M+ revenue) scaling or dissatisfied with generic, "fluff-heavy" agencies. They specifically avoid budget-conscious clients.

**Positioning angle**: Premium and exclusive. They position as the antithesis to cheap, high-volume marketing, restricting their client base to only one practice per 3-mile radius to guarantee market dominance.

**Key messaging themes**:
- **Search-Only Focus**: Ignoring generic social media in favor of capturing high-intent searches (e.g., "sciatica at 9 PM").
- **Exclusivity**: Strict territorial limits (1 practice per 3 miles) to prevent internal competition.
- **Real ROI Tracking**: Focus on "conversion attribution" and booked appointments, framing their higher fees as an investment in patient Lifetime Value (LTV).
