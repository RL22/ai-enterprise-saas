## Positioning & Messaging

**Primary value proposition**: A comprehensive, "full-funnel" partner that consolidates website, SEO, ads, and CRM into one accountable system, eliminating the need for multiple disconnected vendors.

**Target audience**: Practice owners frustrated with managing a patchwork of marketing providers, looking to hand over their entire digital presence to one expert team. Not suited for multi-specialty groups.

**Positioning angle**: Accountability and integration. They focus on an "education-first" strategy, steering clinics away from cheap "new patient specials" and instead building the doctor's authority in the local community.

**Key messaging themes**:
- **Vendor Consolidation**: One accountable partner to prevent finger-pointing between different service providers.
- **Education over Discounts**: Positioning the chiropractor as a local expert rather than competing on price.
- **Full-Funnel Approach**: Handling everything from AI search visibility to automated CRM follow-ups.
